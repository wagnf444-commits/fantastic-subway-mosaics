""Stationary Figures" by William Wegman @ 23rd Street

In a random store, a dog behind the checkout counter

“I wanted to create portraits of individual characters, people who you might see next to you on the platform. For these I dressed the dogs in more or less ordinary clothes, nothing too fashionable. I was very interested in the way in which photographs, even the out of focus dogs in the background of some images, c ould be translated into mosaic by Mayer of Munich, who skillfully turned gray stones into gray dogs.” - William Wegman
At 23rd Street subway staion in new york city, where the F and M lines run through, you will see dozens of dogs take on human attributes, from wearing street clothes like a shiny raincoat or flannel shirt, to being grouped like passengers as they gaze into space or peer down the platform as if waiting for the train. The dogs are Weimaraners, and alike any others they have striking human eyes. This art installation makes me think about the love of dogs in this city, whether its an actual owner or a dog walker, dogs are just as much residents in this city as people.
